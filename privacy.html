Privacy policy

homelab-rclone is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests the drive.file scope, which lets it access only the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It uploads encrypted backup files to that Drive and reads them back when restoring. It does not read, collect or process any other Google user data.
  • No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
